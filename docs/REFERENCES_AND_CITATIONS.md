## 1. 🩺 Clinical & Medical Evidence (Peer-Reviewed Medical Journals)

### The 2-Minute Indian Consultation Crisis
* **Title:** *Consultation length in primary care: a study of 67 countries*
* **Authors:** Greg Irving, Ana Luísa Neves, Hajira Dambha-Miller, et al.
* **Journal:** *BMJ Open*, 2017; 7(10): e017982.
* **DOI:** `10.1136/bmjopen-2017-017982`
* **Direct URL:** [https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/7/10/e017982](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/7/10/e017982)
* **Key Stat to Quote:** India’s average primary care consultation lasts **just over 2 minutes (120–150 seconds)**, among the shortest in the world.

### The 70–80% Diagnostic Value of Clinical History
* **Title:** *Relative contributions of history-taking, physical examination, and laboratory investigation to diagnosis and management of medical outpatients*
* **Authors:** J. R. Hampton, M. J. Harrison, J. R. Mitchell, et al.
* **Journal:** *British Medical Journal (BMJ)*, 1975; 2(5969): 486–489.
* **DOI:** `10.1136/bmj.2.5969.486`
* **Direct URL:** [https://www.bmj.com/content/2/5969/486](https://www.bmj.com/content/2/5969/486)
* **Re-affirmation Study:** Peterson MC, et al. *Contributions of the history, physical examination, and laboratory investigation in making medical diagnoses.* *West J Med.* 1992; 156(2): 163–165. [PubMed Link](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC1002568/)
* **Key Stat to Quote:** Eliciting an accurate clinical history yields the correct primary diagnosis in **70% to 80%** of outpatient cases prior to physical examination or investigations.
